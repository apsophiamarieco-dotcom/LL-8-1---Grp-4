What is Our Solution?

Have a vending-like machine in school, where students can put clean plastic and they can choose what they can have in return, like paper bags, eco bags, and small paper boxes. This encourages students to throw plastic properly, and they can use eco-friendly packaging instead. We will sell the plastic to recyclers, and we will earn money from it so we can buy more eco-friendly packaging. Our machine will decrease plastic use and increase awareness on using eco-friendly packaging.

How Does It Work?

1. A person can throw plastic waste in the machine.
2. They can choose what eco-friendly packaging they want in exchange.
3. The machine will give the eco-friendly packaging.
4. The plastic waste collected will be sold to recyclers.

How Does It Solve the Root Cause?

Root causes are lack of incentives for proper plastic disposal and limited access to eco-friendly packaging. The solution motivates correct waste handling through rewards and provides sustainable alternatives to replace single-use plastics.

Why is This Solution Effective?

The solution is effective because it intrigues people, the exchange for packaging can be exciting for people, making people more inclined to throw away their plastics in exchange for paper.

Decision Matrix Check

  • Easy to Do: Yes, we can start with one machine in a good spot, set up a simple system to sort and store collected plastic, get affordable paper packaging, and communicate how to use it clearly.
  • Affordable: Yes, because our machine has already been made in another country so we won't need to spend money on the trial and error stage and it is also affordable because the reward the people get after donating their single use beverage container they receive a paper packaging in return
  • Will Solve the Problem: Yes, it reduces plastic waste by encouraging proper recycling and offers practical eco-friendly packaging instead of single-use plastics. It also builds sustainable habits.
  • Creative: Yes, it's creative as it is both good for the environment and gets people to throw their plastics in exchange.

    • Expected Outcome

    Less plastic waste, more sustainable paper packaging, and greater awareness for eco-friendly packaging.
