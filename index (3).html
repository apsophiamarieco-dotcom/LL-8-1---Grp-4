Problem

Plastic waste keeps building up because eco-friendly packaging is expensive and not well known.

Proof of the Problem

  • According to Plastic Free July. (2025), plastic waste continues to grow because eco-friendly packaging is costly and not well understood.
  • Eco-Friendly packaging tips: Costs, materials & more | Packsize. (n.d.) stated that eco-friendly packaging is much more expensive than traditional packaging.
  • Boz, Z., Korhonen, V., & Sand, C. K. (2020) found that consumers often avoid sustainable packaging because of high costs.
  • According to Why is sustainable packaging so expensive? (2022), sustainable packaging is more expensive since companies try to balance cost and quality.
  • According to Company, H. M. (2024), both consumers and businesses find eco-friendly packaging too expensive.

    • Root Cause Analysis (RCA)

    Companies still choose plastic because it is cheaper to produce and brings more profit than investing in eco-friendly packaging technology.

    Project Goal

    The goal is to reduce plastic use, as well as, promote the use of eco-friendly packaging.
